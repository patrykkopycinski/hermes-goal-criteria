Blog post plan: "Make your agent's goals fail loudly — the goal-criteria plugin"

Companion deliverable to github.com/patrykkopycinski/hermes-goal-criteria · drafted 2026-09-14 · publish target: personal blog + Hermes Discord #plugins-skills-and-skins

1. Thesis

Agent goal loops complete "on vibes" when criteria are missing or written from a summary of the plan. The fix is structural, not prompt-level: derive the completion bar from the plan artifact itself, and make deferral a budgeted, evidenced exception rather than a free outcome.
